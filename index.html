Clean-Label Backdoor Attacks for Diffusion Models

Hassan Mahmood, Alina Oprea, Ehsan Elhamifar

A meta-learning framework that shapes the poisoned inputs so that standard diffusion training still learns accurate score estimates on them, instead of denoising the trigger away — raising poisoning success by roughly 20% over small-perturbation baselines.

Hassan Mahmood, Ehsan Elhamifar

A compositional framework for targeted multi-label universal adversarial perturbations that cuts time and space complexity from exponential to linear while retaining strong attack effectiveness.

A knowledge-graph based framework for semantically aware multi-label attacks that resolves the negative gradient correlation between target and correlated labels, scaling to large datasets.
